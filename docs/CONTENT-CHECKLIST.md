## Observed usability issues and design response

- The source hides navigation behind a hamburger even at the inspected desktop width. This version displays section links on desktop and a correctly labelled disclosure on smaller screens.
- Pale source headings have weak contrast; important text now uses dark navy on light surfaces or light text over a strong image overlay.
- Repeated centred sections and large mobile gaps flatten hierarchy. An agent introduction, sales feature, paired guidance, gallery, and service columns have distinct roles.
- Source carousel thumbnails are small, and autoplay moves the content. This version has larger controls, selected-thumbnail state, and manual pacing.
- The cookie panel competes with lower-page contact content. This version needs no tracking banner and places the original notice in a contextual footer disclosure.

## Scope and evidence

No testimonials, awards, listings, credentials, statistics, phone numbers, or recipient emails were invented. Added headings are descriptive navigation/editorial copy. Duplicate responsive source markup and slider clones were consolidated. Source-specific runtime chrome is accounted for above rather than copied as a false local service. Contact delivery, brokerage search availability, and map availability remain controlled by their external providers.
